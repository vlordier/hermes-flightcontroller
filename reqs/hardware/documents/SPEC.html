1.1 MIL-Grade EMI Protection SPEC001

The PCB shall include a continuous ground plane on at least one internal layer and utilize 0.25mm minimum track-to-track clearance to meet MIL-STD-461G radiated emissions requirements.

Parent links: REQ001

1.2 Component Derating SPEC002

All MLCC capacitors shall be X7R or better dielectric grade for temperature stability and aging resistance in space/MIL environments.

Parent links: REQ001

1.3 Mechanical Robustness SPEC003

The PCB footprint for connectors shall utilize mounting holes for mechanical relief to ensure solder joint integrity under launch vibration profiles.
